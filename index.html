The community is the sponsor.

Sacred Chronicles has no advertisements inside its films.

The work is made possible by the people it is created for.

Every film begins long before the camera turns on.

Donations are pooled across the whole production rather than assigned to one line of it. This is the sequence they carry.

  1. 01Research

    Months in books, archives and with scholars before a single shot is planned.

  2. 02Writing

    Scripts drafted and checked until the story holds for a modern viewer.

  3. 03Travel

    Routes planned to reach the places where the events actually happened.

  4. 04Filming

    Cinema cameras on the ground, at the sites most people never see.

  5. 05Editing

    Cutting months of footage into a film worth watching twice.

  6. 06Translation

    Subtitles prepared so the same film can serve the whole Ummah.

  7. 07Release

    Published free, for anyone to find years from now.

Our vision

A library of Muslim history worth growing up with.

Our history is filled with extraordinary people, civilisations and places. Sacred Chronicles turns these stories into cinematic experiences people can see, feel and pass on.

Our story

We began by creating what we wished existed.

We grew up surrounded by extraordinary history, but rarely experienced it in a form that could hold our attention. Sacred Chronicles began with a simple belief: the Ummah deserves films made with the same care as the stories they carry.

The work

Every frame, filmed where it happened.

We travel to the places where history unfolded, study the stories carefully and turn them into films that feel present, human and worth remembering.

The future archive

We are building something the next generation can inherit.

An archive a young Muslim can discover years from now, and a family can watch together.
